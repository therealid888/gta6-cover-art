Vice Cover Studio turns your own photos into GTA-style poster cover art: multi-panel layouts, per-panel crop and position, bold Anton display lettering, Vice City sunset and neon colour grades, and one-click PNG export in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline in the browser — photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
Then open http://localhost:8080.
